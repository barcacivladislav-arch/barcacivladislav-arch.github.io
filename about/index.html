Profile

The work changes format. The thinking stays connected.

I’m Vladislav, a senior multidisciplinary designer working across identity, packaging, websites, ecommerce, campaigns and retention.

That range matters because the decisions are connected. Packaging affects ecommerce. Campaigns depend on the brand system. Websites need to support the real business behind them.

I start by understanding the audience, constraints, competitors and recurring patterns. Data gives direction; design judgment turns it into something clear, useful and visually considered.

My background in Marketing and Logistics strengthened how I think about positioning, behavior, process and delivery. I work directly with founders, marketers, creative directors and developers from direction through production and handoff.

I also build reusable templates, design systems and automation structures where they remove friction and help teams continue the work without losing quality.
