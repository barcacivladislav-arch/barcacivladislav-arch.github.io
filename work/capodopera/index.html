(A) — Context

Bringing a premium product portfolio into a premium digital environment.

The scope

Ecommerce, email, paid advertising, seasonal campaigns and retail communication.

The challenge

Capodopera 12 is a Romanian fashion retailer carrying products from more than 80 international brands. The assortment was premium, but the previous homepage offered little beyond navigation, a hero and basic men’s and women’s entry points. It functioned without creating the environment expected from a premium retailer.

My contribution

I redesigned the ecommerce experience around discovery and worked for more than a year across website visuals, email, advertising and seasonal campaigns. Collections, bestsellers, editorial content, brand information and the physical store became part of a richer storefront instead of leaving customers to navigate the catalogue alone.

01

Full-funnel campaigns for different levels of intent.

Campaign direction

Top-funnel creative introduced the campaign and built sale awareness. Middle-funnel communication made relevant categories such as sneakers, T-shirts and trousers easier to enter. Bottom-funnel work focused on individual products and proven bestsellers.

The visual hierarchy changed with the customer’s intent while keeping the products and premium positioning in control.

02

More than a year of fashion communication.

Newsletter system

Email covered seasonal drops, Valentine’s Day, Easter, Christmas, Black Friday, Summer Sale and ongoing commercial campaigns.

The objective was never simply to place a product beside a buy button. Each campaign sold the feeling around the product—style, aspiration, occasion and identity—while remaining recognizably Capodopera.

03

A richer storefront built around discovery.

Ecommerce consistency

Collections, bestsellers, editorial content, brand information and the physical store continue the campaign after the click. Desktop and mobile layouts preserve the same premium rhythm while supporting practical browsing and conversion.

(B) — Outcome

Premium retail communication that can change pace without losing its identity.

  • Connected ecommerce, paid advertising and email around one seasonal campaign logic.
  • Protected a premium visual tone while making promotion and urgency immediately understandable.
  • Built flexible newsletter structures for product curation, editorial storytelling and conversion.
  • Created a richer discovery environment for a portfolio of more than 80 international brands.
