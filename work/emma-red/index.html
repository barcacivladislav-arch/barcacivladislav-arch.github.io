Selected project 14Real estate · Brand identity

Emma Red Estate.

An energetic real-estate identity focused on the connection between people and homes.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
—
Scope
Identity, brand applications, communication
System
Focused case study
(A) — Context

Real estate communication needed warmth without losing authority.

The scope

Identity, brand applications, communication

The challenge

Emma Red Estate needed to stand apart from restrained and impersonal category conventions while still communicating trust and professional confidence.

My contribution

I shaped a high-energy identity and extended it through brand applications and communication surfaces.

01

More human energy in a formal category.

Design logic

The identity uses a distinctive color and direct graphic behavior to make the brand feel active and approachable. Consistent composition allows that energy to scale without becoming visually uncontrolled.

(B) — Outcome

A property identity with more character and a more human voice.

  • A recognizable alternative to conventional real-estate branding.
  • Consistent behavior across identity and communication applications.
  • A flexible foundation for property and campaign content.
