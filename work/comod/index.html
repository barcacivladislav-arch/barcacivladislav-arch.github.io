COMOD.

Bringing a legacy furniture company into the digital era.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
—
Scope
Brand applications, ecommerce design
System
Focused case study
(A) — Context

Bringing a legacy furniture company into the digital era.

The scope

Brand identity, ecommerce strategy, responsive design and implementation-ready delivery.

The challenge

Founded in 1996, COMOD is one of Moldova’s long-established furniture businesses. Its website dated from roughly the late 2000s and eventually disappeared after the domain expired. The opportunity was to give an established offline business a digital identity for the first time.

My contribution

I developed the new identity and translated it into a complete ecommerce experience. The work moved iteratively between Figma and AI-assisted development until the brand, catalogue, services and promotional content formed one coherent system ready for client handoff.

01

Comfortable by design.

Identity direction

The identity is built around the feeling COMOD’s furniture should communicate: comfort. Beige, muted green, purple and blue create a warm foundation, while a restrained orange accent adds energy without making the brand feel inexpensive.

The logo and wider visual system were developed before the ecommerce layer so that product presentation, navigation and campaigns could share one recognizable tone.

02

From almost no digital presence to a complete storefront.

Ecommerce system

The new website brings products, categories, promotions, company information and services into one coherent experience.

I designed and built it through an iterative workflow combining Figma and AI-assisted development, repeatedly moving between design and implementation. My part is complete and delivered; final client content and imagery remain before launch.

(B) — Outcome

A complete digital foundation for an established offline business.

  • Created a new identity around comfort and approachable quality.
  • Translated the brand into a complete catalogue-led ecommerce system.
  • Used an iterative Figma and AI-assisted development workflow to connect design and implementation.
  • Delivered the completed system for final client content, imagery and launch preparation.
