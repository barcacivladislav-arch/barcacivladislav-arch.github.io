Selected project 13Hospitality · Concept identity

Like Coffee.

A compact identity for an everyday coffee ritual.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
—
Scope
Identity, packaging, social direction
System
Focused case study
(A) — Context

An everyday coffee ritual made immediately recognizable.

The scope

Brand identity, packaging and social direction.

The challenge

Like Coffee needed to feel warm, approachable and memorable without relying on an overly complex visual world.

My contribution

I developed the identity and applied it to the most visible hospitality touchpoints, including packaging and social communication.

01
Character → packaging → daily use

A small identity built for frequent use.

Visual system

The system uses a focused set of recognizable elements that can remain consistent from a cup or package to everyday social communication.

(B) — Outcome

A compact identity with enough character to grow.

  • A clear and approachable identity direction.
  • Recognizable behavior across packaging and communication.
  • A focused concept ready for a fuller hospitality system.
