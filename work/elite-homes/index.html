Building an urban hospitality brand from identity to booking.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
2025
Scope
Identity, platform UX, sales material
System
Figma, Illustrator, Photoshop
(A) — Context

An urban home away from home.

The scope

Brand identity, digital product, hospitality communication and booking experience.

The challenge

Elite Homes manages short-term rental apartments in Chișinău. The opportunity was to create something more considered than the typical local rental brand and to position the apartments as fully prepared living spaces rather than places to sleep.

My contribution

I developed the identity around the idea of an urban nest—a home away from home—and translated the balance between urban living and comfort across logo, color, typography, patterns, print, digital applications and the booking platform.

01

An urban nest, expressed as a complete identity.

Brand foundation

The visual system balances urban confidence with the comfort of a prepared home. Logo, color, typography and patterns create a recognizable hospitality language without competing with the apartment photography.

02

From identity to booking experience.

Digital platform

I translated the brand into a platform where guests could discover apartments and choose the right stay. The work covered the homepage, apartment discovery, listing pages, property details, review, payment and confirmation across desktop and mobile.

At the time, the portfolio included more than 40 managed apartments, so reusable components and page rules were essential for keeping the experience coherent as inventory changed.

(B) — Outcome

One hospitality idea carried from identity to booking.

  • Created an urban hospitality identity around the idea of a home away from home.
  • Extended the system across print, applications, property imagery and digital product design.
  • Designed a responsive booking journey for a portfolio of more than 40 managed apartments.
  • Connected homepage, discovery, listing, property, payment and confirmation into one visual journey.
