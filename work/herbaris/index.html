Herbaris.

Repositioning an established Romanian natural-care brand across ecommerce, packaging, advertising and retention.

Role
Brand, web & campaign design
Period
2025—2026
Scope
Ecommerce, packaging, ads, email
System
Creative direction through production
The assignment

Making natural home care impossible to overlook.

The scope

Brand evolution, Shopify ecommerce, packaging, advertising, newsletters and ongoing brand communication.

The challenge

Herbaris is an established Romanian producer of natural detergents, cosmetics and home-care products with more than a decade on the market. The products were already selling, but the dated store, promotion-led advertising, visually inconsistent newsletters and packaging did not communicate why the range was different. Because Herbaris was not competing at the lowest price point, price alone could not be the answer.

My contribution

I led design in close collaboration with the agency’s Art Director across the ecommerce experience, packaging direction, advertising, newsletters and ongoing brand communication. A subcontracted designer handled the brand refinement and later packaging execution, while our team developed the broader creative direction, concepts and packaging moodboards.

01

Turning the package into part of the positioning.

Packaging and product world

The goal was not to turn Herbaris into another quiet beige eco brand. Naturality needed to remain an advantage without dominating every message; the products still had to feel effective, desirable and competitive. The principle was simple: let the products speak.

The previous packaging did not immediately communicate naturality, quality or differentiation. A stronger color system distinguishes categories and fragrances, while custom organic illustrations bring natural cues and character into the range before the customer reads a claim.

The system was adapted across detergents, personal care and cosmetics, from standard consumer sizes to 5L formats. Laundry detergent, kitchen degreaser and shower gel make the transformation visible without needing a long explanation.

02

Rebuilding the ecommerce experience.

Shopify experience

Rather than reskinning the existing store, we rebuilt the Shopify experience across the homepage, collections, product pages, navigation and mobile.

The homepage moved from a conventional storefront to a brand-led shopping experience. Collections help customers understand the range and reach relevant products faster. Product pages put benefits and purchase decisions ahead of visual clutter, while mobile navigation reflects how most customers actually browse.

The work continued after launch. We improved the store around customer behavior and introduced curated entry points such as Bestsellers and Founder’s Favorites for people who did not yet know what they needed.

03

Test broadly. Learn. Focus.

Advertising

The initial advertising system explored many messages and visual directions to learn what attracted customers. Patterns emerged: bestsellers attracted attention, reviews built confidence and strong guarantees reduced hesitation.

Those learnings moved the work from volume toward more focused creative built around proven products, useful benefits and messages customers already understood.

04

Build urgency around messages that already proved their value.

Summer Sale

The Summer Sale brought the strongest acquisition patterns into one concentrated campaign. Product recognition, social proof, clear guarantees and offer-led urgency worked together across email, Meta, Google and the storefront.

05

Turning the first purchase into an ongoing relationship.

Retention and newsletters

Acquisition through Meta and Google was only one part of the strategy. Email became the channel for turning first-time buyers into returning customers.

We developed welcome, browse-abandonment, abandoned-cart and win-back flows alongside evergreen education and larger promotional campaigns. Instead of repeating ecological claims, communication focused on cleaning performance, fragrance, practical advice and products people already loved.

Educational content gave the brand something useful to say between promotions, while bundles, collections and bestsellers created natural opportunities for repeat purchases and higher-value orders.

Campaign inbox.

Every supplied newsletter remains available. The Summer Sale popup is excluded.

Outcome

A stronger brand system, a clearer store and measurable commercial growth.

  • After the expected disruption of migrating to a completely new store, online performance recovered and grew by approximately 20% or more.
  • Ongoing ecommerce improvements contributed to a lower bounce rate and a clearer path through the catalogue.
  • Returning customers represented approximately 60% of customers; this figure should not be attributed to the redesign alone.
  • During the Summer Sale, email generated approximately 8,000 RON in the first two days and Meta plus Google advertising generated approximately 6,000 RON over the same initial period.
  • The campaign period contributed to a record month of approximately 160,000 RON in revenue.
