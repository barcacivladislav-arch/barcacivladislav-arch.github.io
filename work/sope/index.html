Case study 18Body care · Advertising · Retention

SOPE.

Turning body care into a conversation worth opening.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
2026
Scope
Advertising, newsletters, campaign concepts
System
Acquisition & retention creative
The assignment

Making body care feel as distinctive as the scents.

The scope

Paid social advertising, product storytelling, review creative and long-form campaign newsletters.

The challenge

SOPE needed to turn sensory products into clear digital messages without flattening the brand into routine ecommerce. Every asset had to create desire quickly, then make the product, scent or offer easy to understand.

My contribution

I developed a connected creative system across acquisition and retention: strong opening hooks for social, product-led stories for consideration, proof and offers for conversion, and newsletters that carry the same voice through a longer narrative.

01

Make the product felt before asking for the click.

Advertising system

The campaign moves from attention and sensory language into product proof, best sellers and customer reviews. The visual voice stays recognisable while each stage answers a different buying question.

Editorial photography and oversized type introduce the product. Scent stories translate fragrance notes into familiar textures and moods. Offer and review creative then provide specific reasons to buy.

02

Carry the hook into a complete campaign story.

Campaign newsletters

Five long-form newsletters turn short social hooks into product menus, scent education, review proof and collection stories.

Each email keeps the brand voice active while organising a much larger amount of information, extending the same campaign language from acquisition into retention.

Campaign inbox.

Every supplied SOPE newsletter remains available for close reading.

Outcome

A connected campaign language across acquisition and retention.

  • Created twenty campaign assets across attention, consideration and conversion.
  • Extended the same voice into five complete newsletter experiences.
  • Built repeatable structures for product launches, scent stories, offers and customer proof.
  • Kept the communication recognisably SOPE while changing the purpose of each message.
