Intergasses.

Designing a complex B2B ecommerce experience from scratch.

Role
Brand & digital designer
Period
—
Scope
Information architecture, UX, UI, Webflow
System
Focused case study
(A) — Context

Building a B2B ecommerce platform from the ground up.

The scope

Information architecture, wireframes, UX/UI, responsive ecommerce and Webflow-ready design.

The challenge

Intergasses is a Polish supplier of industrial and specialty gases distributed in cylinders across European markets. Customers arrive with different levels of technical knowledge and purchase intent, so the catalogue needed more structure than a conventional product grid.

My contribution

I designed the ecommerce experience from scratch, beginning with wireframes and information architecture before developing the complete desktop and mobile interface. The system covers discovery, technical product information, purchasing and the wider company offering.

01

Wireframes first. Interface second.

Website exploration

The work began with product architecture, navigation and decision paths. Establishing the structure first made it possible to develop a detailed interface without allowing visual treatment to obscure technical relationships.

Homepage, catalogue, product and supporting pages demonstrate a deliberately UX-heavy project: structure, desktop behavior, product experience and mobile adaptation are shown as one connected system.

02

Industrial precision across every responsive state.

Visual system

Typography, spacing, product imagery and technical information follow repeatable rules prepared for practical Webflow implementation. The result supports a growing catalogue without losing clarity or recognizability.

(B) — Outcome

A complex industrial catalogue made easier to understand and buy from.

  • Designed the B2B ecommerce structure from wireframes through complete responsive UI.
  • Created clearer paths through categories, technical product information and purchasing.
  • Built consistent desktop and mobile page logic for a growing specialist catalogue.
  • Grounded the interface in practical Webflow implementation constraints.
