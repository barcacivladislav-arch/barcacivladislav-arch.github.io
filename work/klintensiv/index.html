(A) — Context

Designed for professionals. Communicated like it.

The scope

Campaigns, advertising, print, B2B communication, newsletters and selected packaging.

The challenge

Klintensiv operates across hospitals, medical practices, hospitality and other professional environments. In this category, communication has to do more than attract attention: it must establish trust and make technical information usable.

My contribution

My work focused primarily on advertising, campaign communication and print, with additional work across newsletters and selected packaging. I used one positioning idea to keep very different products, audiences and use cases recognizably part of the same professional brand.

01

Chosen by professionals. Made for professionals.

Campaign system

Rather than treating every campaign as an isolated graphic, this positioning became the foundation for product campaigns, educational materials, promotional offers and institutional communication.

Healthcare, product, educational, promotional and B2B messages could change purpose and tone while maintaining the same professional credibility across continuous, high-volume production.

01. Brand credibility

Make the proof visible.

Manufacturing, laboratory and clinical imagery give the brand’s professional positioning a concrete visual language.

02. Product categories

One system, four use cases.

Colour and application imagery distinguish instruments, nebulisation, hand hygiene and surfaces within one recognisable campaign.

03

The professional voice continues into the inbox.

Newsletters

Email extends the campaign logic into product education, offers and recurring communication, balancing immediate commercial clarity with the trust expected by professional buyers.

(B) — Outcome

A professional communication system built for continuous production.

  • Established a consistent positioning across healthcare, product, educational, promotional and B2B communication.
  • Turned technical and compliance-heavy information into usable campaign and ecommerce hierarchies.
  • Maintained one recognizable voice across advertising, print, newsletters, packaging and website banners.
  • Created flexible structures that support a large and continuously changing volume of work.
